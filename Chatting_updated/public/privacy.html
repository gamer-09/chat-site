Privacy Policy

How ptr_29 Chat handles data

Effective and last updated: August 25, 2026

Important: messages, room passkeys, and uploaded files are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized room participants, the service providers, and the operator may be technically able to access them. Do not share secrets through the Service.

1. Scope and operator

This Policy explains how the operator of ptr_29 Chat (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the production application at gamer-09.github.io/chat-site. It covers the website, anonymous authentication, chat rooms, messages, presence, reactions, read receipts, and uploads.

The application is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Supabase as its authentication, database, realtime, and file-storage provider.

2. Information processed

  • Account data: access requires login or registration at the start gate. We store your username and a password hash — the password is SHA-256-salted on your device, then bcrypt-hashed server-side with a secret pepper — plus avatar and creation time. There is no password-recovery process, and hashes are not readable, listable, or modifiable through the API.
  • Profile data: your chosen username, optional avatar URL, anonymous user/client ID, and last-seen time.
  • Room data: room name, public/private setting, owner, administrators, members, creation time, and any room passkey.
  • Content: messages, replies, edit state, timestamps, reactions, read receipts, files, images, original filenames, MIME type, and file size.
  • Presence and activity: current room, online status, typing signals, unread counts, and notification events. Stale presence records are designed to be pruned after a short period.
  • Browser data: profile preferences, terms acceptance, generated client identifiers, saved room passkeys, unread counts, theme, Supabase authentication session, and service-worker cache.
  • Technical request data: hosting and infrastructure providers may process IP address, user agent, request time, referrer, and diagnostic/security logs when your browser connects.

3. How information is used

  • Authenticate the browser anonymously and maintain a stable identity.
  • Deliver messages and media, synchronize rooms, and show presence, typing, reactions, and read receipts.
  • Enforce room membership, ownership, administration, passkeys, and row-level access controls.
  • Remember local preferences and reconnect the browser to previously joined rooms.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, secure, moderate, and prevent abuse of the Service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and valid legal requests.

There is currently no dedicated advertising or behavioral-analytics integration in the application, and we do not sell personal information.

4. Who can see information

Public rooms

Messages, usernames, avatars, reactions, receipts, and shared media in a public room are available to authenticated visitors who can access that room. Online/offline status and last-seen times of all users are visible in the People panel.

Private rooms

Private rooms use Supabase row-level security, owner/admin/member lists, and passkeys. Once a passkey admits a user, that anonymous user ID may be added as a room member. Private-room content is not end-to-end encrypted.

Room administrators, operator, and providers

Room owner/admin powers apply only to the room they manage. They can manage that room’s settings and membership and can clear or delete the room, but the application does not let them ban a user platform-wide, terminate an anonymous identity, delete another user’s profile, revoke a username, or force a username change.

The Supabase project owner may be technically able to inspect or manually change database and storage records outside the application. This infrastructure access is separate from the room-admin interface and is not an automated global account-management or moderation feature.

GitHub and Supabase process data as infrastructure providers; jsDelivr delivers the Supabase browser library; DiceBear receives avatar requests when a generated avatar is used.

5. Storage, passkeys, and uploads

If you upload a profile picture, it is stored as part of your profile (avatar) and shown wherever your profile or presence appears. Room access lists store participant identifiers only; room settings display owner and admin usernames, not raw identifiers, where resolvable.

Database records are stored in Supabase. Uploaded files are stored in a non-public Supabase Storage bucket. The application requests time-limited signed URLs—currently up to seven days—to display authorized media.

Room passkeys are access-control values, not encryption keys. They may be stored in the Supabase room record and in your browser’s local storage. Anyone who obtains a valid passkey may be able to join the associated room.

Browser local storage is used instead of advertising cookies for application state. Clearing site data can remove your local profile, saved passkeys, preferences, and access to the same anonymous session.

6. Retention and deletion

No fixed automatic retention period currently applies to messages, rooms, profiles, receipts, reactions, or uploads. They may remain until a user deletes an eligible message, a room owner/admin clears or deletes that room, a Supabase project administrator performs a manual data operation, the project is reset, or provider retention rules require deletion.

Renaming or deleting your username performs a full wipe of that name's messages, reactions, and read receipts before the profile change completes. The operator can additionally purge any username (profile row plus its content) at any time.

Presence works as follows: while your browser tab is open you appear online (even without a room); closing the tab removes your presence and lists you offline with a "seen …" last-seen time visible to other users.

Accounts: access to the app requires login. Passwords are SHA-256-salted on your device and then bcrypt-hashed server-side with a secret pepper; password hashes are not readable, listable, or modifiable through the API (the tables have no client-facing policies). Failed logins are rate-limited (5 tries, 15-minute lock). Your chat content is tied to your account id, so it follows you across devices after login.

Deleting your account (the in-app Delete Account control, confirmed with your password) permanently erases the account record, your chat user, every message, reaction and read receipt sent under your name, your presence, and any rooms you own; the username then becomes available for registration again. A wrong password deletes nothing.

Deleting a message or room record may not immediately delete an associated object from storage, cached signed URL, backup, or provider log. We do not promise instant or forensic erasure.

7. Security

HTTPS protects network traffic in transit. Supabase anonymous authentication, row-level security policies, a private upload bucket, and signed media URLs provide access controls. However, no online service is completely secure, and these controls are not end-to-end encryption.

Security also depends on your device, browser, passkey handling, Supabase configuration, third-party services, and other room members. A recipient can copy, download, photograph, or redistribute anything they can view.

8. Browser notifications and local choices

The Service may ask for browser notification permission. Notifications can contain the room name, sender name, and a message preview, which may appear on a locked screen or be visible to other device users. You can deny or revoke permission in browser settings.

You can clear local storage, cookies, service-worker data, and notification permissions through your browser. Doing so may sign you out, remove saved passkeys, or make the same anonymous identity unrecoverable.

9. Third-party services and international processing

The Service relies on GitHub Pages, Supabase, jsDelivr, and DiceBear. Those providers may process information in countries outside your own and under their own privacy terms. Review their policies for details about infrastructure logs, security, retention, and international transfers.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 submitted information, contact the operator with enough detail to locate it, but do not send additional sensitive information.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, or to receive a portable copy. These rights may be limited by the anonymous nature of the Service, other users’ rights, legal obligations, and the ability to verify that a browser identity or content belongs to you.

You can edit your own profile, delete eligible messages, leave rooms, clear browser data, disable notifications, or use the in-app Delete Account control, which performs the full erase described in section 6. For a broader request, contact the operator and provide the minimum information needed to identify the relevant account, room, or message.

The current creator/admin interface cannot terminate another anonymous identity, ban a user across the platform, revoke or reserve a username, or automatically erase every record associated with a person. A request may require a manual Supabase data operation, may be limited by the ability to verify ownership, and may not be technically possible for every provider record or copy.

12. Client-ID requests (consent-based disclosure)

The Service treats your Client ID as private by default: it is never displayed on profiles, member lists, or public pages. A user may request another user's Client ID from the Online Users list (⋮ menu → Request Client ID) by submitting a written reason. The recipient sees the requester's username and the full reason in their Inbox and may approve or deny the request. Approval discloses a snapshot of the approver's Client ID at that moment to the requester only; subsequent identity changes are not disclosed and require a new request. Request records (requester, target, reason, status, timestamp, and any disclosed snapshot) are readable only by the two participants and are retained to preserve a transparent history of disclosures. Do not include passwords, passkeys, or other sensitive information in request reasons. Abuse of the request feature (harassment, pressure, or misuse of disclosed IDs) may be reported via the contact below.
