👤 Your Profile

Click Edit Profile in the sidebar to set your name and avatar. Your Client ID is unique and permanent — keep it safe.

  • ✏️ Username is checked live — you'll see a ✓ when it's available
  • 🖼️ Avatar URL — paste any image link, or leave blank for an auto-generated avatar
  • 🪪 Client ID — share this so room owners can grant you access to private rooms
  • 🗑️ Delete Account removes your profile and all stored data from the server

🏠 Rooms

All available rooms are listed in the left sidebar. Public rooms are open to everyone. Private rooms require a passkey or invite.

  • 📌 Click a room to switch into it — unread count shown in red badge
  • ➕ Create Room — give it a name and optionally make it private
  • 🔒 Private rooms — only accessible with a passkey or if the owner adds you
  • 🔑 Join by passkey — paste the passkey code at the bottom of the sidebar and click Join
  • ↻ Refresh — manually reload the room list

💬 Sending Messages

Type your message in the box at the bottom and press Enter or click Send. Images can be attached with 📷.

  • ↩️ Reply — hover a message and click ↩ to quote it in your reply
  • 📋 Copy — hover a message and click 📋 to copy its text
  • ✏️ Edit — you can edit your own messages within 5 minutes of sending
  • 🗑️ Delete — permanently remove your own messages at any time
  • 🖱️ Right-click (or long-press on mobile) to open the context menu
  • 📷 Images — click the camera button to share a photo from your device

⚙️ Room Settings

Click ⚙️ Settings in the chat header to manage a room. Only the room owner and admins can make changes.

  • ✏️ Rename — give the room a new name (not available for #general)
  • 🔒 Privacy toggle — switch between public and private
  • 🔑 Passkey — generate, copy, or clear the room's passkey. Share it so others can join
  • 🛡️ Admins — add users by Client ID to help manage the room
  • 👥 Members — grant specific users permanent access to a private room
  • 🗑️ Delete Room — permanently removes the room and all messages (owner only)

🟢 Online Users & Info

The right panel shows everyone currently online and which room they're in. The info banner at the top of the chat area shows key room details.

  • 👁️ Online panel — see all connected users and their current room in real time
  • 🔔 Room info banner — shows owner, live user count, and admin count at a glance
  • 📬 Unread badges — red numbers on rooms you haven't read yet
  • 💡 Click "View Info" in the banner to jump straight to the settings panel
  • ⌨️ Typing indicator — see when someone else is typing in real time
  • 🖼️ Click any image in chat to view it full-screen in the lightbox
