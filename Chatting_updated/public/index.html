👋 Welcome to ptr_29 Chat

A real-time group chat with public and access-controlled private rooms, replies, reactions, files, images, and room-scoped management.

  • 🔐 Log in or create your account at the start gate — your username, rooms and content follow the account across devices
  • 🏠 Choose a room from the sidebar; #general is the shared starting room
  • ✉️ Type and press Enter (or Send) to post a message
  • 📱 Use the Rooms, Chat, and Online tabs at the bottom👥 Tap the People button in the room bar to open the People panel👤 The People panel on the right shows who is online
  • 🎓 Guided tour is watch-only: fake volunteers (Nova & Rex) help the guide demonstrate every room control while you just press Next; demo rooms auto-delete afterwards
  • ❓ Navigate this guide with its buttons or the ← and → keyboard keys

👤 Your Profile

Your account (created at the start gate) sets your initial username; Edit Profile lets you change it (the old name's content is wiped) and set an optional avatar.

  • ✏️ Username is checked live and must currently be available
  • 🖼️ Avatar — paste an image URL or tap 📷 Upload picture (works on phones); leave blank for a DiceBear avatar
  • 🪪 Client ID identifies this browser; it stays private unless you explicitly approve sharing it; clearing site data may change it
  • 🗑️ Delete Account asks for your password, then permanently erases the account, the chat user, every message/reaction/receipt under the name, presence, and owned rooms. Renaming still wipes the old name's content.
  • ♻️ Abandoned names (holder offline, or orphaned rows) can be reclaimed by anyone; active names are protected

🏠 Rooms

Available rooms appear in the left sidebar. Public rooms are open to signed-in anonymous visitors. Private rooms require existing membership or a valid passkey.

  • 📌 Click a room to switch to it; unread messages appear as a badge
  • ➕ Create Room with a name and optional private-room setting
  • 🔒 Private means access-controlled, not end-to-end encrypted
  • 🔑 Join by passkey by entering the code in the sidebar; the browser may save it for reconnecting
  • 👥 Membership persists for the same anonymous browser identity after a successful private-room join
  • ↻ Refresh manually reloads the room list
  • 🎓 The guided tour creates two temporary rooms (public + private), lets you browse them as a guest — sending unlocks after registration — and deletes them when it ends

💬 Sending Messages

Type in the bottom box and press Enter or Send. Messages synchronize through Supabase Realtime.

  • ↩️ Reply, 📋 copy, 😊 react, ✏️ edit, or 🗑️ delete from the message actions
  • ⏱️ Edit is shown for your own messages during the first 5 minutes
  • 👆 Tap a message on mobile or right-click it on desktop to reveal actions
  • 📎 Files can be up to 25 MB; 📷 images can be up to 10 MB
  • ✓ Read receipts and typing status are visible to room participants
  • ⚠️ Deleting a message removes it from chat, but an associated uploaded storage object may remain

⚙️ Room Settings

Click ⚙️ Settings to manage the current room. Owner and admin permissions apply only to that room.

  • ✏️ Rename the room (not available for #general)
  • 🔒 Privacy switches between public and access-controlled private membership
  • 🔑 Passkey can be generated, copied, saved, or cleared; it is not an encryption key
  • 🛡️ Add Admin / Add User manage room powers and private-room access; settings show owner & admin usernames (not raw IDs)
  • 🧹 Clear removes the room’s message rows; Delete Room is available to the owner or a room admin
  • 🚫 No global moderation: room admins cannot ban a user platform-wide, terminate an identity, delete another profile, or revoke a username

🔐 Privacy, Data & Current Limits

The production site uses GitHub Pages and Supabase. Understand what the current application does before sharing content.

  • 🆔 Your identity follows your account — log in on any device and your content is there; passwords are SHA-256-salted on-device, then bcrypt + pepper server-side; the name “Anonymous” is reserved
  • 🌙 Theme — switch dark/light with the 🌙/☀️ toolbar button; your choice is remembered
  • 🔒 Private rooms are not end-to-end encrypted; authorized participants and infrastructure providers may technically access content
  • 🗑️ Delete Account (password-verified) erases account + user + every message/reaction/receipt + presence + owned rooms; renames wipe the old name; the operator can purge any username
  • ⚪ Presence & last-seen are visible to other users while your tab is open and as a “seen …” time after you leave
  • 🛡️ Admin rights are room-scoped; there is no global ban, account termination, or username-revocation control
  • 📄 Read the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy for full details

👤 Profiles & Client-ID Requests

In Online Users, tap the ⋮ button next to a person and choose View profile or Request Client ID. Profiles show their avatar, live status and public activity — never their Client ID.

  • 📊 Profiles show public activity only: presence, message/room/image/reaction stats, first & last active
  • Client IDs are never shown on profiles — they stay private by default
  • 🔑 You may request someone's Client ID with a written reason; they see who asked and why
  • ✅ Only an explicit approval shares the ID (a snapshot at that moment — later changes need a new request)
  • ❌ Denials are final for that request; you may ask again later, but never pressure anyone
