① Set your profile

At the bottom of the Rooms sidebar, tap Edit Profile to choose a username and avatar URL. The app checks in real-time that your chosen name isn't already taken.

  • 🆔 Your Client ID is a unique identifier. Share it with room owners so they can add you as an admin or member.
  • 🔤 Username must be 2–50 characters
  • 🖼️ Paste any image URL for a custom avatar
  • ✅ A green tick means the name is free

④ Private rooms & passkeys

In ⚙️ Settings, toggle a room to Private, then generate a passkey. Share that key with anyone you want to invite — they paste it in the sidebar to join.

  • 🔐 Passkeys are saved so you don't re-enter them
  • 👁️ Private rooms only appear to members
  • 🔄 You can change or clear the passkey any time

⑤ Admins & members

As the room Owner, open ⚙️ Settings → View Info to add or remove admins and members by their Client ID. Admins can manage the room but can never remove the owner.

  • 🆔 Your Client ID is shown under your username
  • 🛡 Admins can delete rooms, clear messages, add members
  • 👥 Members have access to private rooms without a passkey
